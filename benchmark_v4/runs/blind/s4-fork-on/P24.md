For working with Sense HAT and GPIO, you should use the following:

- **Device Profile**: `pi_sensehat`, `pi_gpio`
- **Image**: `ghcr.io/chameleoncloud/edge_sensehat_image:latest`

These settings are suitable for a Raspberry Pi 4 (64-bit) device (`raspberrypi4-64`) with an ARM64 architecture. This configuration is specifically designed to support Sense HAT and GPIO functionalities on the CHI@Edge testbed of Chameleon Cloud.
