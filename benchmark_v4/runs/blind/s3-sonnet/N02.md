I don't have access to a Raspberry Pi or its camera — I'm running in a cloud sandbox with no camera hardware attached, so I can't record or download video for you directly.
What I can do is give you the exact command to run on your Pi. Assuming you're using libcamera-vid (Raspberry Pi OS Bullseye+ with the standard camera stack):
bashlibcamera-vid -t 10000 --width 1280 --height 720 --framerate 30 -o video.h264

-t 10000 — duration in ms (10 seconds)
--width 1280 --height 720 — 720p
--framerate 30 — 30 fps
-o video.h264 — output file

If you want an MP4 container instead of raw H.264, pipe through ffmpeg or use:
bashlibcamera-vid -t 10000 --width 1280 --height 720 --framerate 30 -o video.mp4 --codec libav
Then to get it onto your machine, from your local terminal (not the Pi):
bashscp pi@<pi-ip-address>:/path/to/video.mp4 ./
(replace pi@<pi-ip-address> with your Pi's actual username/IP, and adjust the path).
If you're using the older raspivid tool instead (Legacy camera stack), let me know and I can give you the equivalent command.
