To record a 5-second 1080p video from the camera, you can use the rpicam-vid utility. Here's an example of how to do it:

cmd = "rpicam-vid --nopreview -t 5000 --output /app/video1.h264 --width 1920 --height 1080 --framerate 30"
result,code = my_container.execute(cmd)
print(result)

This will capture a 5-second 1080p video at a framerate of 30 fps and save it to a file named video1.h264 in the /app directory of the container.

Please note that the --nopreview option is used since there's no display attached to the device, and the --framerate 30 option is used to set the framerate to 30 fps.

After capturing the video, you can download it from the container using the container.download() method:

my_container.download("/app/video1.h264", ".")
