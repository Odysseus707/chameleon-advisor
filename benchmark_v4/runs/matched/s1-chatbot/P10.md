To tear everything down, you need to remove the CHI@Edge container and free the device.

Here are the steps to follow:

Delete the container:
my_container.delete()

Delete the lease:
my_lease.delete()

These steps will remove the container and free the device, effectively tearing down the setup.

Note: These steps assume that my_container and my_lease are the variables used to create the container and lease, respectively, as shown in the provided reference material.
