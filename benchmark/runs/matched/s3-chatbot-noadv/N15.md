To reserve any free Raspberry Pi 5 on CHI@Edge by machine type for 3 hours, follow these steps:

1. **Log in to Chameleon Cloud**: Go to the [CHI@Edge site](https://chi.edge.chameleoncloud.org) and log in with your credentials.

2. **Choose Project and Site**:
   - Ensure you are working within the correct project.
   - Select CHI@Edge as your site from the dropdown menu at the top left corner of the Horizon dashboard.

3. **Navigate to Device Calendar**:
   - On the left side, click on "Reservations" > "Leases".
   - Click on “Device Calendar” and change the vendor type to “Raspberry Pi”.

4. **Identify Available Raspberry Pi 5 Devices**:
   - Look for devices named in the format `nyu-rpi5-XX` where XX is a number.
   - Identify an available two-hour block that fits within your desired time frame, but since you need a 3-hour reservation, ensure there are no other reservations overlapping with your intended start and end times.

5. **Create Lease**:
   - Click on “Create Lease” from the left side menu under "Reservations" > "Leases".
   - Set the lease name to something meaningful (e.g., `rpi5_lease_netID` where `netID` is your actual net ID).
   - Specify the start date and time in UTC, ensuring it starts on an hour boundary.
   - Modify the lease length until you have a 3-hour duration. For example:
     - Start Time: `XX:00`
     - End Time: `(XX+2):59` (to ensure full 3 hours).

6. **Reserve Devices**:
   - Click “Next”.
   - On the "Devices" tab, check the box to reserve devices.
   - Set the minimum and maximum number of hosts to `1`.
   - In the "Resource properties", specify the UUID of a Raspberry Pi 5 device you identified earlier.

7. **Submit Lease**:
   - Click “Create” to submit your lease request.

8. **Verify Lease Status**:
   - Check that your lease status shows as “Pending”. Once it transitions to “Active”, you can proceed with launching containers or other tasks on the reserved Raspberry Pi 5 device.

By following these steps, you will have successfully reserved a free Raspberry Pi 5 for 3 hours on CHI@Edge.
