To tear down everything and free up resources in CHI@Edge, follow these steps:

1. **Delete the Container**: Remove the container that was launched for the experiment.

    ```python
    my_container.delete()
    ```

2. **Delete the Lease**: Free up the device by deleting the lease you created earlier.

    ```python
    my_lease.delete()
    ```

These commands will remove the CHI@Edge container and free the reserved device, ensuring that resources are available for other users or future experiments.
