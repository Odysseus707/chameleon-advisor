For Sense HAT / GPIO work on CHI@Edge, you should use specific device profiles and images depending on whether you are working with the Raspberry Pi Sense Hat or the Waveshare Sense Hat.

- **Raspberry Pi Sense Hat**: Use the `pi_sensehat` device profile and the image `ghcr.io/chameleoncloud/edge_sensehat_image:latest`.
  
- **Waveshare Sense Hat**: Use the `pi_gpio` device profile and the same image `ghcr.io/chameleoncloud/edge_sensehat_image:latest`.

These configurations ensure that all necessary dependencies and device interfaces are available for interacting with the sensors on your chosen Sense Hat.
