To tear down everything and free up resources in CHI@Edge, you need to delete both the container and the lease that was created for your experiment. Here are the steps:

1. **Delete the Container**: Use the `delete` method on the container object.

    ```python
    my_container.delete()
    ```

2. **Delete the Lease**: After deleting the container, use the `delete` method on the lease object to free up the device.

    ```python
    my_lease.delete()
    ```

These steps will remove the CHI@Edge container and release the reserved device back into the pool for other users or future experiments.
