For Sense HAT and GPIO work on CHI@Edge, you should use the following:

- **Device Profile**: `pi_sensehat` for Raspberry Pi Sense Hat or `pi_gpio` for Waveshare Sense Hat.
- **Image**: Use the image `ghcr.io/chameleoncloud/edge_sensehat_image:latest`.

This setup ensures that your container has access to all necessary dependencies and device profiles required to interact with the sensors on both types of Sense HATs.
