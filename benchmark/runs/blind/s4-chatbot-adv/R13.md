Right now, you can use a `raspberrypi4-64` machine type with an ARM64 architecture to read temperature and humidity from a Sense HAT on CHI@Edge for about 3 hours.

Top 3 device types to reserve:
1. **Raspberry Pi 4 (64-bit)** - Best suited as it directly supports the required Sense HAT image and has the necessary device profiles.
2. **Jetson Nano** - Offers good performance and power efficiency, though you may need to ensure compatibility with the Sense HAT image.
3. **Raspberry Pi 3B+** - A reliable fallback option if Raspberry Pi 4 is unavailable, but it's 32-bit so ensure the image supports this architecture.
