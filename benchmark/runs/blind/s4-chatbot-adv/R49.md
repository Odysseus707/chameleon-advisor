Currently, CHI@Edge offers devices such as Raspberry Pis and Jetson Nanos/Xaviers with GPUs. For your workload of running a CUDA object-detection model on a live camera stream for about 4 hours, here are my top three device types to reserve:

1. **Jetson Xavier**: Best choice due to its powerful GPU capabilities which can handle complex CUDA operations efficiently.
2. **Jetson Nano**: A good alternative if the Xavier is not available; it also has a GPU but with less power compared to the Xavier.
3. **Raspberry Pi 4 (64-bit)**: Suitable for simpler tasks or if more CPU-based processing is acceptable, though it lacks a dedicated GPU.

These devices can be reserved via any supported testbed interface and used just like Chameleon-provided devices.
