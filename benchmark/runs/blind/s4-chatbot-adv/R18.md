Currently, you can use a `raspberrypi4-64` machine type with the image `ghcr.io/chameleoncloud/edge_sensehat_image:latest`, which supports reading temperature and humidity from a Sense HAT for about 3 hours.

Top 3 device types to reserve:
1. **Raspberry Pi 4 (64-bit)** - Best suited as it directly supports the required Sense HAT image.
2. **Jetson Nano** - Offers good performance with ARM architecture, though may require additional setup.
3. **Odroid C2** - Another ARM-based device that could support the workload but might need custom configuration for optimal use.
