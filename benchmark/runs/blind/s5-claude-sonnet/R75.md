No availability listing was provided with this request, so I cannot tell you what's currently free on CHI@Edge — check the Horizon host-calendar or `openstack reservation list --site CHI@Edge` before reserving.

Ranked best first for real-time video analytics that needs tensor cores, 1 device for about 4 hours:

1. jetson-agx-orin-devkit-64gb — the most powerful tensor-core-equipped board on CHI@Edge (Ampere architecture), giving the highest throughput and best headroom for real-time, possibly multi-stream, video analytics.
2. jetson-orin-nano-devkit-nvme — also Ampere with tensor cores, lower power and smaller footprint than the AGX Orin, a solid choice for single-stream real-time workloads if the AGX Orin isn't available.
3. jetson-xavier-nx-devkit-emmc — Volta GPU with 48 tensor cores; older and less powerful than the Orin family but still genuine tensor-core hardware, adequate for real-time analytics at lower resolution or frame rate.

I would not recommend jetson-nano (Maxwell GPU, no tensor cores), raspberrypi4-64 or raspberrypi5 (no GPU acceleration suited to this), or coral-dev (Edge TPU accelerator, not tensor cores) for a workload that specifically requires tensor cores.
