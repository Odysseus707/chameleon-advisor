For capturing stills from an attached Pi camera module, the key constraint is the CSI camera connector that the official Raspberry Pi Camera Module plugs into — that connector and its driver stack (picamera2/libcamera) are specific to the Raspberry Pi boards, not the Jetson or Coral devices, whose camera connectors use different modules/adapters that I'm not confident are Pi Camera Module compatible. So I'd stay within the Raspberry Pi family:

1. raspberrypi4-64 — has the standard CSI camera port, is well documented for libcamera/picamera stills capture, and is the most commonly available CHI@Edge Pi variant.
2. raspberrypi5 — also has a CSI camera connector and the newer libcamera stack; note the Pi 5 uses a different/smaller CSI ribbon-cable connector than the Pi 4, so make sure your camera module's cable (or an adapter) matches before you commit the reservation.
3. raspberrypi3 — older and slower, but still has a CSI port with mature camera support; 3 hours is plenty of time even at its lower performance, so it's a reasonable fallback if the newer Pis are unavailable.

I would not recommend a Jetson or Coral device for this specific task unless you've independently confirmed your camera module is compatible with their camera connector — I don't know that for certain, so I'm not listing them.
