I don't have visibility into live CHI@Edge availability from here, so I can't confirm current reservation status for specific devices — check the Blazar/Horizon dashboard, `blazar` CLI, or python-chi's device-availability query before reserving.

Based on general CHI@Edge hardware capabilities (not live state):

1. jetson-agx-orin-devkit-64gb — the most capable Jetson on CHI@Edge (Ampere GPU, 64GB unified memory), giving the most headroom for CUDA fine-tuning even though the model is small, and it's the safest choice if you're unsure of exact memory needs.
2. jetson-orin-nano-devkit-nvme — newer Ampere-generation GPU with solid CUDA/cuDNN support in a smaller footprint, well matched to fine-tuning a small vision model without needing AGX-class capacity.
3. jetson-xavier-nx-devkit-emmc — Volta GPU with dedicated tensor cores and mature CUDA support; a reasonable fallback if the Orin-class devices are unavailable, though slower to train on than the two above.

Note: raspberrypi4-64, raspberrypi5, and coral-dev are not CUDA-capable (no NVIDIA GPU) and should be ruled out for this task regardless of availability. jetson-nano is CUDA-capable but is an older, memory-constrained (4GB) part — I'd only fall back to it if none of the above are free.
