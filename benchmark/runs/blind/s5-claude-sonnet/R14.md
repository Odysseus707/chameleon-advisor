This task is gated by form factor, not compute: a Sense HAT is a Raspberry Pi HAT requiring the standard 40-pin GPIO header, and it physically stacks on a Raspberry Pi board only — it won't fit or work on a Jetson or Coral device.

1. raspberrypi4-64 — standard 40-pin GPIO header the Sense HAT plugs into, with well-documented I2C/Sense HAT support on the CHI@Edge Pi 4 image for reading its HTS221 temperature/humidity sensor.
2. raspberrypi5 — same 40-pin header and I2C bus; a reasonable alternative if the Pi 4 isn't free, but verify the sense-hat library/kernel overlay ships on the Pi 5 image, since I'm less sure of parity there.

No third option: none of the other CHI@Edge device types (jetson-nano, jetson-xavier-nx-devkit-emmc, jetson-orin-nano-devkit-nvme, jetson-agx-orin-devkit-64gb, coral-dev) have the GPIO header a Sense HAT needs. If neither Pi board is free, this task simply can't be done on CHI@Edge right now.
