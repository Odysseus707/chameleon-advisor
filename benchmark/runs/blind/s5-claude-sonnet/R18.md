The binding constraint on this task is hardware form factor, not compute power: a Sense HAT requires the standard Raspberry Pi 40-pin GPIO header and physically stacks on a Raspberry Pi board only.

1. raspberrypi4-64 — has the 40-pin GPIO header a Sense HAT needs, with well-documented I2C/Sense HAT support on the CHI@Edge Pi 4 image for reading its HTS221 temperature/humidity sensor.
2. raspberrypi5 — same GPIO header and I2C bus; a workable alternative to the Pi 4, though confirm the sense-hat library/overlay ships on the Pi 5 image, since I'm less certain of parity there.

No third option: none of the other CHI@Edge device types (jetson-nano, jetson-xavier-nx-devkit-emmc, jetson-orin-nano-devkit-nvme, jetson-agx-orin-devkit-64gb, coral-dev) have the GPIO layout a Sense HAT requires. If neither Pi board is free, this task can't be completed on CHI@Edge right now.
