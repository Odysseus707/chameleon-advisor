Assuming the relevant Jetson-family boards are free, here is my ranked recommendation for a CUDA object-detection model on a live camera stream, 1 device, ~4 hours:

1. jetson-agx-orin-devkit-64gb — highest CUDA core count and INT8/FP16 throughput of any CHI@Edge board, so it has the most headroom for real-time detection plus video decode overhead.
2. jetson-orin-nano-devkit-nvme — Ampere-generation CUDA cores with current JetPack/TensorRT support at a much smaller power/cost footprint than the AGX Orin, sufficient for most single-camera detection models.
3. jetson-xavier-nx-devkit-emmc — Volta-generation CUDA plus a dedicated DLA, a well-proven combo for real-time detection (YOLO/SSD-class models), though slower than either Orin board.

I would not recommend jetson-nano for this — its older Maxwell GPU and limited memory bandwidth make sustained live-stream CUDA inference marginal. coral-dev, raspberrypi4-64, and raspberrypi5 don't expose CUDA at all (Coral relies on its Edge TPU, not CUDA; the Pis have no discrete GPU), so none of them can satisfy a "CUDA" requirement.
