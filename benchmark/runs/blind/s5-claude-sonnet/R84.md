I don't have visibility into live CHI@Edge device availability, so I can't tell you what's actually free right now — check the device/host calendar in Horizon or query availability via `python-chi` before you reserve anything.

Based on general suitability for running an int8 model on a hardware accelerator for a single ~3-hour reservation, here's my ranked recommendation:

1. **jetson-orin-nano-devkit-nvme** — Ampere-generation GPU plus a dedicated DLA give strong native INT8 throughput through TensorRT, and it's the newest/most capable Orin-class board CHI@Edge offers for this kind of workload.
2. **jetson-xavier-nx-devkit-emmc** — Volta GPU with two NVDLA engines has long-established, well-tested INT8 support in JetPack/TensorRT, a safe and proven choice for a short single-device job.
3. **coral-dev** — the Coral Dev Board's Edge TPU is an ASIC purpose-built for int8-quantized TensorFlow Lite models; if your model can be converted to that format it will be extremely fast and power-efficient, though it's narrower in framework support than the Jetson boards (TFLite/int8 only).

If none of these turn out to be free when you check, `jetson-agx-orin-devkit-64gb` and `jetson-nano` are the other accelerator-class device types on CHI@Edge worth checking as fallbacks — I can't vouch for their current reservation state either.
