Currently, CHI@Edge offers several device types that can be used for running a small vision model with CUDA for about 8 hours on one device. Here are my top three recommendations:

1. **raspberrypi4-64**: This is the best choice as it provides an ARM-based architecture and has sufficient compute power to handle most small vision models, though note that Raspberry Pi devices do not have dedicated GPUs with CUDA support.
   
2. **iot-rpi-cm4-02** (Waveshare Sense Hat device): This device also uses the Raspberry Pi Compute Module 4 which is similar in capabilities to the raspberrypi4-64 and can be used for running small vision models.

3. **iot-rpi4-picam2** (Raspberry Pi Sense HAT device): Another variant of the Raspberry Pi 4, this device includes a Sense Hat but otherwise has comparable specifications to the standard Raspberry Pi 4 model.

Please verify the availability of these devices as they may not always be free and available for reservation.
