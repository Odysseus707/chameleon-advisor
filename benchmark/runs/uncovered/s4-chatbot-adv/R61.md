Currently, CHI@Edge offers several device types that can be used for running a small vision model with CUDA for about 8 hours on one device. Here are my top three recommendations:

1. **raspberrypi4-64**: This is the best choice as it provides an ARM-based architecture and has sufficient compute power to handle small vision models, though note that Raspberry Pi devices do not have dedicated GPUs.
2. **jetson-nano**: This device offers a more powerful GPU compared to the Raspberry Pi 4, making it suitable for running CUDA-accelerated workloads efficiently.
3. **jetson-xavier-nx**: With even greater computational capabilities and a robust GPU, this is ideal if you need higher performance but may be overkill depending on your specific requirements.

Please verify the availability of these devices as they are recommended based on typical use cases and might not always be free or available in real-time.
