To drive GPIO pins and read an I2C sensor on CHI@Edge, you can use a Raspberry Pi 4 device since it supports these functionalities. Here are my top three recommendations for device types to reserve:

1. **raspberrypi4-64**: This is the best choice as it provides full support for GPIO pins and I2C sensors, making it ideal for your experiment.
   
2. **iot-rpi4-picam**: While primarily designed for camera usage, this device also supports GPIO and I2C functionalities, though you may need to verify its availability.

3. **raspberrypi3-64**: This is a fallback option if the Raspberry Pi 4 devices are not available. It still offers support for GPIO pins and I2C sensors but has less powerful hardware compared to the Raspberry Pi 4.

For all these options, ensure you have the necessary device profiles or configurations that expose the required /dev nodes for GPIO and I2C operations.
